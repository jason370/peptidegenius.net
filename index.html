Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

PeptideGenius ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the PeptideGenius application ("the App").

1. Data Storage

PeptideGenius is designed with privacy at its core. All user data — including peptide inventories, shot logs, vial tracking, supply counts, and titration plans — is stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal health data on any external server.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Personal health or medical information
  • Injection logs or dosing data
  • Peptide inventory details
  • Location data
  • Contact lists or phone data
  • Photos, videos, or files

3. Information We May Collect

If you choose to upgrade to PeptideGenius Pro, we may collect:

  • Email address: Used to deliver your license key and communicate about your account.
  • Payment information: Processed securely by our payment provider (Stripe). We never see or store your credit card details.

If you contact us via email at PeptideGenius@gmail.com, we retain your email address and message content to respond to your inquiry.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Stripe: For secure payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy can be found at stripe.com/privacy.
  • Netlify: For hosting the web application. Netlify's privacy policy can be found at netlify.com/privacy.

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

5. Cookies and Tracking

PeptideGenius does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any form of user tracking. We do not serve advertisements.

6. Data Security

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you are in full control. We recommend using your device's built-in security features (passcode, biometrics) to protect access to your data. The App's built-in backup feature allows you to export your data as a JSON file for safekeeping.

7. Children's Privacy

PeptideGenius is not intended for use by children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
